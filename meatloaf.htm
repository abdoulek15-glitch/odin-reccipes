Meatloaf Recipe

This is a very easy and no fail recipe for meatloaf

Ingridents

  • 2/3 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 1/2 pound ground beef
  • 2 eggs, lightly beaten
  • 1/4 cup onions, minced finely
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon sage, dried
  • Dash of pepper

Sauce Ingridents

  • 1/4 cup ketchup
  • 1/4 teaspoon nutmeg
  • 1 teaspoon mustard
  • 3 tablespoons brown sugar
Direction
  • preheat oven to 350F
  • Combine the loaf ingredients in a large bowl and mix them together well, making sure to incorporate the egg and the breadcrumbs thoroughly.
  • Press the mixture firmly into a greased loaf pan,and smooth out the top with the back of a spoon.
  • In a small bowl, whisk together the sauce ingridents and spread evenly pver the loaf.
  • Bake at 350F for 50 mintues. Allow to cool for at least 10 mintues prior to serving
  • Plate up the piping-hot goodness alongside potatoes and green peas or asparagues for a perfect and easy springtime supper with soul.
